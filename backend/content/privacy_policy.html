ApolloSG Privacy Policy

Effective June 2026 · ApolloSG is a brand of Harmony Wellness Group (HWG), Australia

In short: ApolloSG checks things on your phone first. It has no account, no advertising tracking, and it never sells your information. When ApolloSG uses AI tools to check something for you, your personal information is removed before anything is sent to them.

1. Who we are

ApolloSG is a scam-protection app made by Harmony Wellness Group (HWG), which is accountable for how ApolloSG handles information and for the services it uses. You can contact us at support@harmonywellnessgroup.com.au.

2. What ApolloSG looks at on your phone

ApolloSG examines the messages, links, phone numbers, screenshots and files you choose to check, on your phone. Passwords, verification codes and other secrets are found and removed on your phone before anything is sent. Your precise location, browsing history, contacts and hardware identifiers are not collected.

3. What is sent to ApolloSG's service

  • Checks you ask for: the privacy-processed content of the item you submit (for example a suspicious message, link, phone number or screenshot), so it can be investigated.
  • Your device: a random device ID issued by ApolloSG, the app version, a coarse time-zone offset and your quiet-hours settings. There is no account, name or email sign-up.
  • Alerts: a push-notification token, if you turn notifications on.
  • Optional features: email addresses you ask ApolloSG to watch for data breaches; a read-only Gmail connection if you connect one; and the names, contact details and alerts you choose to share with family members you pair with.
  • Patrol summaries: the category, result, time and website domain of each check. Full messages, screenshots and files are not kept in these summaries.

4. Use of AI tools

ApolloSG uses AI tools to investigate possible scams, explain results in plain English and answer your questions about staying safe.

  • Your personal information is removed first. Before anything is sent to an AI tool, ApolloSG removes your personal information, including passwords, codes and other secrets, and the personal details you have saved in the app. Personal identifiers in research questions are replaced with labels such as “[phone number]”.
  • Only the scam evidence is sent. The AI tool receives the evidence needed to judge whether something is a scam, such as the wording of a suspicious message, the link, or the sender's details.
  • Not used for training. ApolloSG uses paid AI services whose terms say the information sent is not used to train their models.
  • Short-lived copies. ApolloSG closes its own working copies as soon as each request finishes, and never later than 15 minutes.

5. Other services ApolloSG uses

Each service receives only what it needs for its job:

  • Website safety checks (Google Safe Browsing) and domain registries receive the cleaned web address or domain being checked.
  • Phone-number reputation (IPQualityScore) receives the number being checked.
  • Breach checks (XposedOrNot, or Have I Been Pwned when configured) receive the email address you asked to check.
  • Attachment malware checks (VirusTotal) receive file fingerprints (hashes) only, never the file itself.
  • Notifications and email: push-notification services deliver alerts, and an email delivery service sends family invitations and alerts you set up.
  • Hosting: a database hosting provider stores the records described in this policy.

Some of these services may process information outside Australia. HWG may add or replace services; they remain subject to the same privacy controls, and this policy will be updated where a change materially affects your information.

6. How long information is kept

  • Working copies of a check: closed when the check finishes, and never kept longer than 15 minutes.
  • Ask Higgins chat on your phone: up to 1 hour, and at most the last 40 messages.
  • Evidence for your cases stays on your phone until you delete the case or delete all your data.
  • Patrol summaries, watched email addresses, family links and device records stay until you remove them or delete all your data.

7. Your choices

  • Every protection is optional and can be turned off in the app at any time.
  • You can disconnect Gmail, unpair family members and turn off notifications.
  • You can see what ApolloSG stores and delete all your data from Settings › What ApolloSG stores.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the information kept on your phone. It does not erase records on ApolloSG's service, so please use “delete all your data” first.

8. Access, correction and complaints

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you. Email support@harmonywellnessgroup.com.au. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when ApolloSG's handling of information changes, and show the new effective date at the top.
